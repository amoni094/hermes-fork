# Autonomous Food Retail Vending

Use when researching, planning, or advising on autonomous vending machine businesses for fresh food or beverages (fresh-squeezed juice, fresh produce) — hardware options, market landscape, unit economics, AU regulatory context, and deployment strategy.

Research note: no prior session history for this topic found in Hermes fork profile (Sep 2026). Seeded from fresh web research.

## Fresh-Squeezed OJ Vending: Market Landscape

Full detail: `references/fresh-juice-vending-landscape-2026.md`

Key facts:
- Autonomous fresh-squeezed OJ vending is mature and commercially proven.
- Dominant format: cup (open-top or heat-sealed). Bottle-fill machines exist and are deployed in European supermarkets.
- The resealable-bottle gap: machines that fill into a bottle (customer caps manually) exist (Oranfresh OJ Fill Up, deployed at Rewe Germany). No machine found that autonomously fills AND mechanically recaps a screw-cap bottle.
- Main players: Oranfresh (Italy, originator, 6,000+ units), iJooz (Singapore, 1,500+ units, franchisee model), JusFres (Canada), Vingoo (China, 3,000+ units), Chinese OEM (Konmax, JW Intelligent, NewSaier, TOTEM).
- Chinese OEM capex: ~USD $5,500-7,500 off-shelf. Oranfresh: list price not published, quote required.

## Research workflow for vending machine domains

1. Search confirmed commercial deployments first (iJooz, Oranfresh, JusFres) — gives real unit economics, locations, franchise terms.
2. Search Chinese OEM marketplaces (Alibaba, ecer.com, accio.com) for capex benchmarks.
3. Search industry press (vendingmarketwatch.com, vendingtimes.com, vendingconnection.com) for commentary.
4. Check manufacturer sites directly — Oranfresh product pages load; Tridge insight links may 403.
5. AU-specific: FSANZ regulatory classification is the key gate before committing to any sealed-container product design.

## Adversarial review discipline for product landscape research

- Blog is not a product: an industry blog reviewing a machine is not a named product source. Cite it as confirming a format or workflow only. Concrete: VMFS USA (Aug 2026) reviewed an unnamed bottle-fill machine — format validation, not a product entry.
- Unpublished prices are unpublished: if a manufacturer does not publish list prices, say quote required. Do not estimate or fabricate a range.
- Industrial component cost is not vending module cost: industrial screw-cap torquers exist at known price ranges but a single-lane food-grade vending miniaturisation is custom engineering. Do not extrapolate.
- EU deployment is not AU regulatory clearance: Oranfresh OJ Fill Up at Rewe Germany does not settle FSANZ classification or council DA requirements in AU.
- AU market presence is a research gap: confirm iJooz/JusFres/Oranfresh AU presence before advising on locations or competitive positioning.
- Inferred friction is not validated friction: customer acceptance of manual-cap step is inferred from EU commercial success, not from user satisfaction data. Hedge appropriately.

## AU regulatory context

- Fresh juice dispensed into customer-capped bottle = food service (fresh-prepared). FSANZ Standard 3.2.2.
- Machine that autonomously fills AND seals the container may shift to packaged food under FSANZ Standard 1.1.1 — different date marking, labelling, and potentially different council DA and food business registration category.
- Get AU food regulatory specialist confirmation before committing to auto-seal product design.
