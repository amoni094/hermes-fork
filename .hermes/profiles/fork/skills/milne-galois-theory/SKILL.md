## Overview

Milne's Fields and Galois Theory (v4.61) is a concise graduate text covering field extensions, separability, normality, and the Galois correspondence. Freely available at jmilne.org. Rigorous treatment with exercises.

- Computing Galois groups via discriminants and resolvents
- Solvability by radicals: equivalence with solvable Galois group
