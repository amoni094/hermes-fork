## Overview

Knapp's Basic Algebra (2006) is a comprehensive graduate text covering group theory, ring theory, module theory, multilinear algebra, and structure of fields. Designed for first-year graduate algebra. Companion to Advanced Algebra.

## Structure / Chapters

- Chapter 1: Preliminaries about Integers, Polynomials, and Matrices
- Chapter 2: Vector Spaces over Q, R, and C
- Chapter 3: Inner Product Spaces
- Chapter 4: Groups and Group Actions
- Chapter 5: Theory of a Single Linear Transformation
- Chapter 6: Multilinear Algebra
- Chapter 7: Advanced Group Theory
- Chapter 8: Ordinary Representations and Characters
- Chapter 9: Fields and Galois Theory
- Chapter 10: Modules over Noncommutative Rings

## Key Techniques

- Sylow theorems and group classification
- Jordan and rational canonical forms
- Character theory and Burnside's theorem
- Galois correspondence theorem
- Tensor products and exterior algebras

## Anti-patterns

- Skipping foundational chapters when prerequisites are assumed
- Applying results outside their domain of validity (check assumptions)
- Confusing syntactic and semantic levels in formal treatments
