## Overview

Milne's Group Theory (v3.16) is a concise graduate reference covering basic group theory through Sylow theorems, solvable groups, and free groups. Freely available at jmilne.org. Compact but complete.

## Structure / Chapters

- Chapter 1: Basic Definitions and Results
- Chapter 2: Free Groups and Presentations
- Chapter 3: Automorphisms and Extensions

- Chapter 5: The Sylow Theorems; Applications
- Chapter 6: Subnormal Series; Solvable and Nilpotent Groups
- Chapter 7: Representations of Finite Groups

## Key Techniques

- Sylow theorems: existence, conjugacy, number of p-subgroups
- Burnside's lemma for counting orbits
- Composition series and Jordan-Holder theorem
- Derived series and solvable groups
- Character theory basics for finite groups

## Anti-patterns

- Confusing syntactic and semantic levels in formal treatments
