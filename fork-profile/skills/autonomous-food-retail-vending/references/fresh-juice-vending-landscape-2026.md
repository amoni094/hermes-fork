# Fresh-Squeezed OJ Vending Machine Landscape (September 2026)

Research context: autonomous fresh-juice vending business planning. AU deployment context.

---

## Market overview

The autonomous fresh-squeezed OJ vending market is commercially proven and dominated by cup-format machines. Bottle-fill machines exist and are commercially deployed in European supermarkets (FMCG channel). No machine found that autonomously fills AND mechanically recaps a resealable screw-cap bottle — the reseal is always customer-executed after machine fill.

---

## Key players — confirmed commercial deployments

### Oranfresh (Italy, since 1987)
Originator of the category. 6,000+ units sold globally, 50+ countries. Internationally patented VSS (Vertical Squeezing System).

Product line — bottle-format relevant:

- **OJ Fill Up**: Fills freshly squeezed OJ into bottles (plastic, PLA, or glass) in multiple sizes. Supermarket/FMCG channel. Customer removes cap, hooks bottle to filling head, pays, machine squeezes and fills (~30 sec), customer screws cap back on. Deployed at Rewe supermarkets in Germany. Machine does NOT auto-recap. Free-vend mode supported (customer pays at cashier).
- **OJ Take Away**: Heat-seals a foil lid onto a cup. Sealed single-use cup — NOT a resealable bottle. For transit/passing-through locations.
- **OJ 130**: Open-top cup. Best-seller. Locations: schools, gyms, hospitals, offices, malls, airports.
- **AJ Fill Up**: Same bottle-fill concept for apple juice. Supports plastic, PLA, glass bottles.

Contact: oranfresh.com | info@oranfresh.com | +39 095 29 12 33
List prices not published — quote required.

### iJooz (Singapore, founded 2016)
1,500+ machines in Singapore. Franchisee expansion: Malaysia, Middle East, GCC, Japan, global. AIoT + machine vision + robotics. Price: SGD $2-3/cup. Mobile app (Nov 2024, ~10% of sales). Format: sealed cups (not bottles). Capacity: 125 sealed cups (10-12 oz). Claimed franchisee margin 30-60%, ROI 8-10 months.
URL: ijooz.com

### JusFres (Canada)
40 machines in Toronto + 25 in Vancouver. Transit-focused (TTC subway, SkyTrain). CAD $4.99/serving. Format: cup (open). 100% natural claim.

### Vingoo Juice (China, founded 2010)
3,000+ machines in 176 Chinese cities (2017 data). 4M cups/month. Format: sealed cup. ~RMB 10 (~$1.50 USD). 40-second serve.

## The resealable bottle gap

| Capability | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Fill + automated screw-cap recapping | NOT FOUND off-shelf | No product confirmed Sep 2026 |

The "resealable" in bottle-fill machines is customer-executed, not machine-executed.

## Australian deployment considerations

**Regulatory (FSANZ):**
- Fresh juice dispensed into customer-capped bottle = food service (fresh-prepared). FSANZ Standard 3.2.2 (Food Safety Practices and General Requirements).
- Machine that autonomously fills AND seals the container may shift to packaged food under FSANZ Standard 1.1.1 — different date marking, labelling, and potentially different council DA / food business registration category. Needs AU food regulatory consultant confirmation.
- EU Rewe deployment does not settle AU FSANZ classification.

**AU competitive landscape:** Not confirmed in Sep 2026 research. Verify whether iJooz, JusFres, or Oranfresh OJ Fill Up operate in AU before committing to location strategy.

**Pricing context:** AU fresh cold-pressed juice from juice bars runs ~$7-10 AUD for 400-500 mL. Bottle-format vending supports premium pricing if positioned as take-home, not impulse drink.

**Oranfresh AU distributor:** Not confirmed. Export to 50+ countries — contact info@oranfresh.com for AU inquiry.

## Deployment location fit by format

| Format | Best locations |
|---|---|
| Open-top cup | Offices, gyms, hospitals, schools — drink immediately |
| Sealed cup | Airports, transit — spill-resistant, single-use |
| Filled bottle (customer caps) | Supermarket exits, grocery, gyms — take-home, premium pricing |
| Auto-capped bottle (not off-shelf) | Packaged-goods channel — requires custom build + regulatory review |

---

## Recommended path for AU deployment

1. Verify AU competitor presence and location availability before committing to machine type.
2. Start with Oranfresh OJ Fill Up or Chinese OEM bottle-fill variant (lower capex, EU-proven format).
3. Validate that customer-capping step is not a friction point in your specific AU locations before spending on custom auto-capping engineering.
4. Pursue custom auto-capping only if: throughput is limited by the cap step at high volume, OR regulatory classification requires factory-sealed container, OR moving to labelled/shelf-stable packaged product line.
5. For custom capping: approach Konmax or JW Intelligent with a unit volume commitment and spec. No reliable cost estimate without supplier quotes.
