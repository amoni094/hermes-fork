# Melbourne PPOR Suburb Profiles — Research Reference (August 2026)

Context: High-income PPOR buyer (~$220-250K/yr, 47% marginal rate), budget $1.2M (can stretch),
10+ year hold, family school-zone considerations. Base: July 12, 2026. Last patched: August 31, 2026.

---

## Budget Reality Check

**At $1.2M budget (with stretch)**, freestanding 4BR houses are accessible in:
- Cheltenham (primary recommendation)
- Murrumbeena (McKinnon zone east streets)
- Eltham (outer, lifestyle trade-off)

Most inner/middle-ring SE suburbs remain above budget for houses; units/townhouses only in those.

---

## SRL East Corridor Note

SRL East stations: **Cheltenham → Clayton → Monash → Glen Waverley → Burwood → Box Hill**
Opening: ~2035. None of the typical Frankston-line school-zone suburbs (McKinnon, Ormond,
Bentleigh, Glen Huntly, Carnegie, Murrumbeena) are on the SRL. They are ~3–8km from
Cheltenham terminus. SRL corridor uplift plays belong in Clayton, Glen Waverley, Box Hill.

## Key Pitfalls for Suburb Research

### Data source discrepancies
- **Cotality AVM median** vs **REIV transacted median** vs **REA past-year median** can differ by
  $200K–$400K for small suburbs. Always cross-reference with recent settled sales, not just AVM.
- **MELBZ "median house"** figures sometimes reflect 3BR settled sales only (not all houses). 
  The $863K Murrumbeena figure is this case — treated as 3BR entry price, not all-house median.
- **CRMBrain "median sale price"** may include all property types including commercial — verify.

### School zone verification is mandatory
- Zone boundaries run through individual streets — one side of the street can be different zone.
- Verify EVERY specific address at **findmyschool.vic.gov.au** before purchase.
- Zone boundaries are updated annually — check the year relevant to your child's starting year.
- "Near McKinnon SC" ≠ "in McKinnon SC zone" — agents will blur this distinction.

### Selective school addresses don't matter
MacRobertson Girls', Melbourne High, Nossal, Suzanne Cory all admit by competitive exam.
Home address is completely irrelevant for selective entry — any suburb qualifies equally.
Only local zone schools matter for address-based school selection strategy.

### Inner East caution (July 2026)
Clifton Hill and surrounding Inner East suburbs are showing the weakest auction results in
Melbourne (47.8% clearance). Watch this region before committing.

### SRL corridor — correct suburbs
The SRL East uplift applies to: Cheltenham, Clayton, Monash, Glen Waverley, Burwood, Box Hill.
NOT to Frankston Line suburbs even though they are geographically close to Cheltenham.
