## Stamp Duty — Victoria (2026)

Victoria has Australia's **highest stamp duty** at the top end (6.5% rate kicks in at ~$960K+).

### Standard Rates (PPOR, non-FHB)

| Property Value | Stamp Duty |
|---------------|-----------|
| $600,000 | ~$28,070 |
| $700,000 | ~$37,070 |
| $800,000 | ~$43,170 |
| $900,000 | ~$49,070 |
| $950,000 | ~$52,070 |
| $1,000,000 | ~$55,000+ |
| $1,200,000 | ~$78,000+ |

### First Home Buyer Concessions
- ≤ $600,000 → **$0** stamp duty (full exemption)
- $600,001–$750,000 → sliding scale (e.g. $700K FHB pays ~$24,747 vs $37,070 standard)
- > $750,000 → full standard rates (no FHB benefit)

⚠️ **The $600K/$750K thresholds were set in 2017 and have NEVER been raised.**
At $950K median house, FHB concession is irrelevant for most Melbourne house purchases.

### Off-the-Plan Concession — EXPIRES 20 OCTOBER 2026
Available for contracts signed 21 Oct 2024–20 Oct 2026. Stamp duty calculated on land
component only (not total price). Example: $750K off-plan apartment with $300K construction
component → duty on $450K → FHB pays $0. **This stacks with FHB exemption — genuinely valuable.**

### Total Transaction Costs (PPOR $950K, non-FHB)
- Stamp duty: ~$52,000
- Legal/conveyancing: ~$2,000-$4,000
- Building inspection: ~$600-$1,200
- Mortgage setup: ~$500-$1,500
- Moving/misc: ~$1,000-$3,000
- **Total buying costs: ~$56,000-$65,000**

Selling costs when eventually selling:
- Agent commission (2-2.5%): ~$19,000-$23,750 on $950K
- Legal: ~$2,000-$3,000
- **Total buying + selling: ~$77,000-$92,000 in transaction costs**

Property must appreciate by ~$80K before nominal break-even.

## PPOR CGT Exemption — The Critical Tax Advantage

**PPOR = Section 118-110 ITAA 1997 = 100% CGT exempt on sale.**
No partial discount — full exemption regardless of marginal rate or holding period.
**Unaffected by the 2027 CGT reform** — PPOR exemption remains complete.

### Value at 47% Marginal Rate

Example: Buy $950K, sell 10 years later after 4% growth/year.
- Sale price: ~$1,406,000. Capital gain: ~$456,000.
- CGT as PPOR: **$0**
- CGT if same gain were on investment property or shares (50% discount, 47% rate): ~$107,000
- **The PPOR exemption is worth ~$107K in the above scenario** — this is real after-tax wealth.

This is the decisive reason why generic "invest instead of buy" analyses UNDERSTATE the PPOR
case for high earners. Those analyses model investment property CGT (23.5% effective rate) but
PPOR is 0%.

### Mortgage Interest (PPOR): NOT Deductible
- Interest cost on PPOR loan is fully post-tax (no deduction)
- At $800K loan @ 6%: ~$48K/year interest paid from after-tax income
- Investment property equivalent at 47% rate: would save ~$22K/year in tax → NOT available for PPOR
- This is a genuine PPOR disadvantage vs investment property (absorbed by CGT exemption benefit over time)

## 2017–2019 Downturn: What Marked the Bottom

### Cycle Summary
- Peak: Late 2017 (~$850K median house)
- Trough: Mid-2019 (~10-12% correction from peak)
- Duration: ~18-24 months
- Cause: APRA investor lending caps + Banking Royal Commission (not an economic recession)
- Recovery trigger: Morrison re-election (May 2019) + APRA loosening (June 2019) + RBA cuts (June, July 2019)

### Bottom Signals from That Cycle
1. Auction clearance rates stabilised then recovered from ~40-47% lows
2. Days on market peaked and began declining
3. APRA formally removed investor lending caps (June 2019)
4. Vendor discounting narrowed
5. New listings declined as vendors withdrew
6. Premium inner suburbs recovered first, then filtered through

### Current Cycle Position vs 2017-2019
- 2017-19 was regulatory-driven (could reverse quickly)
- 2026 is rate-cycle-driven (slower to reverse; requires RBA action)
- Current clearance rates: 54-57% (2017-19 worst was 40-47% — so possibly further to go)
- Recovery trigger requires RBA cuts, not expected until mid-2027

### Leading Indicators to Watch NOW (July 2026)
| Indicator | Current | Bottom Signal |

## Decision Framework for High-Income PPOR Buyer (July 2026)

### Arguments for Waiting (rent 6-12+ more months)
1. Still in active price decline (-1.3%/month); Domain forecasts -4% to -8% further
2. Clearance rates show no floor yet (54-57%, falling from 77% a year ago)
3. Every 5% fall on $950K = $47,500 savings; easily worth waiting a few months
4. RBA rate cut (the recovery catalyst) not expected until mid-2027
5. Listings still rising = continued buyer leverage
6. Capital preservation: $100-200K savings is limited buffer vs. transactional costs

### Arguments for Buying Now or Next 6-12 Months
1. CGT exemption worth ~$100K+ over 10 years at 47% rate — not available from investing
2. Can't time the bottom — history shows it's never clear in real time
3. Rent rising in tight market (1.5% vacancy); CBRE forecasts 24% rent growth by 2030
4. Melbourne is cheapest major capital — relative value vs Brisbane/Perth/Adelaide/Sydney
5. Structural undersupply building (approvals at 20-year lows)
6. At $220K income, can service loan without financial stress despite high rates

## Negative Gearing & CGT Reform — PPOR Impact (Law as of June 2026)

Legislation enacted: Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 and Income Tax Rates
Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 — registered 26 June 2026. Changes effective 1 July 2027.
Full detail: references/negative-gearing-cgt-reform-2026.md

**For PPOR buyers — direct impact is ZERO:**
- PPOR CGT exemption (s118-110 ITAA 1997): **UNCHANGED**. Still 100% exempt.
- Negative gearing changes: apply to INVESTMENT properties only
- CGT discount replacement: applies to gains on INVESTMENT assets only

**Indirect benefits for PPOR buyers:**
- Investor demand for established properties fell post-announcement (CBA: ~3% lower prices)
- Less investor competition at auction = more negotiating room for owner-occupiers
- Lock-in effect: pre-Budget grandfathered investors LESS likely to sell (selling loses grandfathering)
  → supply tight in desirable suburbs despite softer demand

**What changed for investors (context only):**
- Established residential properties bought post-Budget night (12 May 2026): losses quarantined
  from 1 July 2027 (can't offset salary; carry forward against property income/gains only)
- New builds: fully exempt — still negatively gearable before and after July 2027
- CGT: 50% discount replaced with cost-base indexation + 30% minimum tax on real gains
- Grandfathered: pre-Budget properties keep old rules until sold

**PPOR remains the most tax-advantaged property holding structure in Australia.**
At 47% marginal rate, a $456K gain on a $950K PPOR (10yr, 4%pa) saves ~$107K in CGT vs
an equivalent investment property. Post-2027 reform this advantage INCREASES because investment
property CGT treatment gets worse while PPOR stays at zero.
