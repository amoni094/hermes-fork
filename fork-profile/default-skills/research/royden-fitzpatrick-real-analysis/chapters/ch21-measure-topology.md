# Chapter 21: Measure and Topology

## Core Idea
On locally compact Hausdorff spaces, Radon measures are the natural class of measures compatible with the topology. The Riesz-Markov theorem identifies the dual of C(X) as the space of signed Radon measures. Regularity properties ensure measures behave well with respect to compact sets and open sets.

## Key Concepts
- **Locally compact Hausdorff space**: Every point has a compact neighborhood. Examples: ℝⁿ, open subsets of ℝⁿ, compact Hausdorff spaces.
- **Radon measure**: Borel measure μ on locally compact Hausdorff X that is (1) locally finite: every point has a neighborhood with finite measure, (2) inner regular: μ(E) = sup{μ(K): K⊆E compact}, (3) outer regular: μ(E) = inf{μ(U): U⊇E open}.
- **Cc(X)**: Continuous functions with compact support. Dense in Lp(X,μ) for 1≤p<∞.
- **Positive linear functional**: L: Cc(X) → ℝ with L(f) ≥ 0 whenever f ≥ 0.

## Key Theorems

### Riesz-Markov Theorem (§21.4)
**Statement**: Let X be a locally compact Hausdorff space. Every positive linear functional L on Cc(X) is represented by a unique Radon measure μ: L(f) = ∫_X f dμ for all f ∈ Cc(X).

- **Use when**: Identifying a linear functional as integration against a measure.
- **Hermes**: If skill scoring is a positive linear functional on continuous routing maps (with compact support on the skill space), it is represented by a Radon measure — the "skill importance measure."

### Riesz Representation for Dual of C(X) (§21.5)
**Statement**: For X compact Hausdorff, [C(X)]* (all bounded linear functionals) ≅ space of signed Radon measures, with norm = total variation.

- **Consequence**: Every bounded linear scorer on continuous functions of context/skill type is a signed Radon measure. Positive scorers are Radon measures; general scorers are differences of Radon measures.

### Regularity of Baire Measures (§21.6)
**Baire σ-algebra**: Generated by compact Gδ sets (countable intersections of open sets). Every Baire measure is regular and extends uniquely to a Radon measure.

## Key Takeaways
1. Radon measures = the right measure class for locally compact spaces: they respect the topology (inner/outer regular, locally finite).
2. Riesz-Markov: positive linear functionals on compactly-supported continuous functions are always measures — a constructive way to build measures from linear algebra.
3. [C(X)]* = signed Radon measures for X compact Hausdorff: measure theory and functional analysis are unified here.
4. Regularity: Radon measures are "well-behaved" — they concentrate on compact sets and can be approximated from inside by compact sets and from outside by open sets.
