# Small Business Operations

Use when the user asks about running or building a small business — staffing, revenue channels, regulatory compliance, partner equity, or operational setup. Australian context unless stated otherwise.

## Business context: Mariam's cleaning business

Mariam is a sole trader running a residential cleaning business in Australia. The user is her admin/operations partner. See `references/mariam-cleaning-business.md` for accumulated context across sessions.

## Procedure: answering a staffing or operations question

1. Check `references/mariam-cleaning-business.md` for accumulated business context before answering — Mariam's structure, targets, and constraints are already established.
2. Default to Australian regulatory framing (Fair Work, ABN contractor model, NDIS rules if relevant).
3. Give concrete platform names, dollar figures, and decision criteria — not generic advice.
4. Flag the contractor-vs-employee distinction whenever hiring is discussed; it has compliance and cost implications.
5. Offer to draft job ads, templates, or scripts as a natural follow-up.

## Staffing: finding domestic cleaners in Australia

Broadest reach (job boards):
- Seek, Indeed, Jora — post casual cleaner role at $28–35/hr. High volume.
- Gumtree — good for ABN contractors who clean on the side.
- Facebook Jobs / local suburb Facebook groups — fast turnaround, underrated.

Platforms with pre-screened candidates:
- Airtasker — can hire directly or poach good performers from completed tasks.
- Hipages, ServiceSeeking — skew toward ABN contractors.

Direct sourcing (fastest for the first hires):
- Ethnic/community WhatsApp and Facebook groups (Filipino, Brazilian, Indian communities have large pools of experienced flexible cleaners).
- Church and community noticeboards if Mariam has those networks.
- Referrals from first hires — cleaning circles are tight; ask every new hire to refer others.

For NDIS-targeted roles:
- Advertise "NDIS experience preferred" on Seek/Indeed; pre-filters for reliability.
- Cleaners need: NDIS Worker Orientation Module (free, ~1–2 hrs online) and NDIS Worker Screening Check.
- Police check also required (~$50–100).

What to screen for:
- Own ABN or willingness to get one (contractor model is simpler initially).
- Reliable transport (non-negotiable for residential rounds).
- References from prior cleaning work.
- Willingness to do police check and NDIS modules if NDIS work is on the table.

## Pitfalls

- Don't skip the contractor-vs-employee question. ABN contractors must genuinely set their own hours and work for multiple clients — if they look like employees (set hours, exclusive engagement, uniforms), Fair Work liability applies.
- The referral flywheel only starts after the first 2–3 reliable hires; push Seek/Facebook hard initially, then switch to referral-first once the core team is in place.
- NDIS Worker Screening Check takes days to weeks. Do not roster a new hire on NDIS clients until the check clears.
